# Income-tax Rules, 2026 — commencement and scope

The Income Tax Department's notice dated 20 March 2026 states that the Central
Board of Direct Taxes made the Income-tax Rules, 2026 under section 533 of the
Income-tax Act, 2025 (Act 30 of 2025), and that the Rules take effect on
1 April 2026.

The department's official help material identifies Tax Year 2026-27 as the
period beginning 1 April 2026 under the Income-tax Act, 2025. It also explains
that earlier tax years and transitional proceedings may remain governed by the
Income-tax Act, 1961. Refer to the department's current statutory material for
the exact treatment of a particular period.

This document establishes statutory identity and commencement only. It does
not establish tax slabs, deductions, rebates, capital-gains treatment, or any
investment-product tax classification. Retrieval must abstain on those matters
unless a separate current, manifest-verified source covers the requested rule.
